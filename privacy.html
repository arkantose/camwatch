CamWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

CamWatch is a personal app used by a single person, its owner, on their own computer.

Google user data

  • CamWatch requests only the drive.file permission. It can see and manage only the files it creates itself (motion clips and snapshots in a "CamWatch" folder). It cannot read any other files in your Drive.
  • Those files are uploaded from the owner's computer to the owner's own Google Drive, and deleted after 30 days.
  • No Google user data is shared with, sold to, or sent to anyone else. There is no server other than the owner's computer and Google Drive itself.

Other data

Video, snapshots and settings stay on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google Drive. CamWatch has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
